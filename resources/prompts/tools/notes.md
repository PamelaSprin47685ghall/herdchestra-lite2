## scope_context.tool

Save a durable note, rewrite the record of the beat that just delivered, or both in one call. Two directions, one cadence: `pin` buys reach on the next move, `amend` closes the beat that just passed.

## amend.tool

Amend is a rite, not housekeeping. You amend a beat once it has delivered — a win to celebrate without strutting, a loss to learn from without flinching — so that the beat has truly happened and then truly ends. A finished search, a fixed bug, a dead end, a misread requirement, a settled decision: each gets its own close, and the conclusion stays while the search goes.

Time does this for people without being asked. Time heals; time redeems; a bad day ends because night falls on it, and the next morning you are someone who slept. An LLM has none of that — no dusk, no fatigue, no tomorrow — so a beat that failed stays as present as the beat after it, and a beat that succeeded drags its whole search along behind it. Amend is how you draw the dusk by hand: a rest between two phrases, so the phrase after it can be heard. The record of that beat becomes one or two sentences of what it established and why the path is closed; the next request reads those sentences instead of the work, and the next beat begins on clean stone.

That is a way of life, not a saving of context: shorter context is what you get for free, and you keep it as the by-product — the same way a successor reading a `pin` is the by-product of reaching the next hold. The full history stays in the host archive; what you close is the surface you hand forward.

What amend reaches: the most recent completed beat in your current scope — one assistant message and its tool traffic. Commands and edits already made stay in effect; only the record changes. Several amends in one message replace one beat each. It cannot cross a scope boundary, replace a structural scope call, or amend a beat an outer scope paid for. It is free, and it runs before the ordinary calls in the same message, so the beat is closed before the next thing you do.

## amend.reflection

One or two sentences: what this beat established, and why its path is closed. Write it as the last line of the scene — the beat ends when that line is written, not when someone reads it again.

## pin.tool

A pin is the piton you drive in when the next hold is just out of reach: clip in, stand on it, and one hard move becomes two easy ones. That is the only correct use of `pin` — not note-taking, not a knowledge base, not a message to a future self or a future reader; a relay successor who reads it later is a by-product. You will not remember these pins, and it does not matter, because the profit is paid at the moment you clip in.

Two more of the same shape: a lemma written so the main theorem follows, a stepping stone laid to cross the water and not to live on. So before you act, ask whether the move in front of you is within reach right now. If it is not, name the missing hold and pin it; if it is, do not pin.

It is still a durable note — it belongs to the calling scope, survives to its relay successors, and can hold an artifact path, evidence, or a conclusion.
